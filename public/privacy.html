Glow Below: privacy policy

Glow Below is a game for young children. It collects no personal data, from children or grown-ups.

What the game keeps

Progress (what you lit, the friends you found) and settings (sound and volume) are saved on your device only, so you can carry on where you left off. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or starting over in the grown-ups' corner, erases them.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads and no in-app purchases
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party services
  • No links out of the game
  • No camera, microphone, location or contacts
  • The app makes no network requests while you play

Where this applies

The Glow Below apps on Google Play and the App Store, and the web version.
