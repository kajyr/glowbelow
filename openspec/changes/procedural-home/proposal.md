## Why

Midnight home is a small hand-built room (3×2 screens) that is the same in every game. A bigger world that is laid out differently for each new game gives a child a reason to come back and explore again. Doing it now, before Twilight, means every later zone is generated by the same system from the start, rather than being hand-built and converted later.

Depends on: `iteration-1-playable-demo` (archived). Comes before `iteration-2-twilight`.

## What Changes

- Midnight home becomes about 8 screens wide and 4 screens tall (about 3× today's size in each direction)
- **BREAKING** (for layout only): the hand-placed layout in `src/levels/midnightHome.ts` is replaced by a generator. Each zone has a fixed recipe of what it contains (start spot, seabed, cave with dumbo, rock arch, shaft, rocks, floating rocks, glow plants, sparkle trails). The positions of these things and the shape of the terrain are generated from a seed
- The generator produces the existing `Level` data shape, so collision, rendering and friends work unchanged
- Fixed rules every generated layout must follow, checked by a validator:
  - no maze and nothing locked
  - the squid can never get stuck
  - every plant and the cave can be reached
  - the cave entrance and the shaft are clear
  - gaps are at least about two squid widths
  - the cave is a reasonable distance from the start
- A layout that breaks a rule is rejected and the next seed is tried. Tests run hundreds of seeds
- The seed belongs to the world: the same seed always gives the same home. A new seed is drawn when a new game starts. Saving it comes with `iteration-3-save-and-album`. `?seed=` in the URL picks a seed for testing
- More guidance for a bigger zone:
  - sparkle trails toward the cave and toward groups of unlit plants
  - more plants, scaled to the area (about 45)
  - sparkle count scaled to the area
- Local lights are limited to the ones near the camera, because about 45 lit plants exceed the 32 light slots. (`iteration-2-twilight` planned the same culling; it moves here.)

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `zone-generation`: seeded generation of a zone from a recipe, the rules every layout must follow, rejecting bad seeds, and when the world changes (new game only)

### Modified Capabilities

- `midnight-home`:
  - "Zone layout": 3×2 hand-made → about 8×4 generated, crossing time updated, cave distance from the start
  - "Glow plants that stay lit": about 20 → about 45 plants
- `sparkles`: "Sparkle trails lead somewhere": trails also lead toward groups of unlit plants across the larger zone, and the first trail starts at the squid

## Impact

- **Code:**
  - new generator and validator in `src/levels/` (pure, tested)
  - `src/levels/midnightHome.ts` becomes a recipe instead of a placement list
  - `main.ts` picks the seed
  - `render/localLights.ts` adds culling
  - the sparkle field and pulse targets already scale with the level data
- **Models:** no new models expected. More rock and seabed variants may be added if layouts look repetitive at the larger size
- **Plan:**
  - `iteration-2-twilight` should generate Twilight with the same system and drop its own light-culling task
  - `iteration-3-save-and-album` saves the seed with the world state
- No new dependencies
