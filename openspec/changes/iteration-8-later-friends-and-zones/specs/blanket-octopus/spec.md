## Purpose

The blanket octopus is an elegant friend with a long flowing cape who invites the squid to ride a long current with it, side by side.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Drifting in open water
The blanket octopus SHALL drift slowly in open water above the Shipwreck, its long cape rippling behind it.

#### Scenario: Seeing it
- **WHEN** the squid swims up from the Shipwreck
- **THEN** the blanket octopus can be seen drifting with its cape rippling

### Requirement: Found by glowing
A pulse reaching the blanket octopus SHALL make it unfurl its cape, play its sound and send a sticker to the album button.

#### Scenario: Unfurling
- **WHEN** a pulse reaches the blanket octopus for the first time
- **THEN** it unfurls its cape and its sticker is added to the album

### Requirement: Long current ride
After being found, touching the blanket octopus SHALL start a long current that carries both of them on a looping ride through the open water and back, as defined in `currents`.

#### Scenario: Ride together
- **WHEN** the squid touches the found blanket octopus
- **THEN** both are carried along a long looping current, side by side

### Requirement: Clearly different from the hero
The blanket octopus SHALL use colours and a cape shape clearly different from the vampire squid (pink and amber, long trailing rather than webbed), so the two are never confused.

#### Scenario: Side by side
- **WHEN** the two ride the current together
- **THEN** each is easy to tell apart by colour and shape
