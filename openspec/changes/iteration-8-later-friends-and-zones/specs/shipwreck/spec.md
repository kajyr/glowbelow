## Purpose

The Shipwreck is an adventure zone beside Twilight: a friendly sunken ship with rooms to peek into, home to the coconut octopus.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shipwreck layout
The Shipwreck SHALL be about 3 screens wide and 2 screens tall, joined to the side of Twilight by a wide passage, with a sunken ship lying on the bottom. The ship SHALL have at least three rooms the squid can swim into through wide openings.

#### Scenario: Peeking into a room
- **WHEN** the squid swims through a porthole-sized opening into the ship
- **THEN** it enters a room and can always swim back out the way it came

### Requirement: Friendly, not spooky
The ship SHALL look cozy rather than scary: rounded shapes, sea green, rust and gold tones, soft light through the portholes and small fish living inside.

#### Scenario: First look
- **WHEN** the squid enters the Shipwreck
- **THEN** the ship looks warm and inviting with nothing scary

### Requirement: Things to light
Each room SHALL contain old lanterns that light up when reached by a pulse and stay lit.

#### Scenario: Lighting a room
- **WHEN** a pulse reaches a room's lanterns
- **THEN** they light up and the room stays lit

### Requirement: Shipwreck extras
The Shipwreck SHALL contain five starfish and one bubble-ring chain.

#### Scenario: Album row
- **WHEN** the album's starfish page is opened
- **THEN** it shows a row for the Shipwreck with five starfish
