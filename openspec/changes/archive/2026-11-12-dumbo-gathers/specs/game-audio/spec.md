## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Dumbo has a signature sound
Dumbo SHALL have its own soft flute-like call, played when it is found and when it points. When dumbo gathers a sparkle, a single short, soft flute note SHALL play, quieter than the squid's sparkle notes and from the same pentatonic scale.

#### Scenario: Dumbo found
- **WHEN** dumbo pops out of the cave
- **THEN** its flute-like call plays

#### Scenario: Dumbo gathers
- **WHEN** dumbo collects a sparkle
- **THEN** one soft flute note plays, and nothing plays when the game is muted
