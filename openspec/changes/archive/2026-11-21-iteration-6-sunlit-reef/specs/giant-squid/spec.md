## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: The way up
Touching the awake giant squid's offered arm SHALL let the squid ride it up through Twilight's top passage and set it down in the Sunlit reef. Steering away during the ride SHALL hop off. After setting the squid down, the giant squid SHALL sink back to its spot in Whale fall, and the squid swims back down by itself. The ride SHALL be the only way into the Sunlit reef; this is the one place where a friend gates progress.

#### Scenario: Express lift
- **WHEN** the squid touches the offered arm
- **THEN** the arm carries the squid up and sets it down in the Sunlit reef

#### Scenario: Hopping off
- **WHEN** the player steers away during the ride
- **THEN** the squid hops off where it is and swims on

#### Scenario: Going home
- **WHEN** the giant squid has set the squid down
- **THEN** it sinks back to Whale fall, where it can be ridden again
