## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The phantom jelly's sounds
Touching the phantom jelly SHALL play a soft, low boing, different from the bump boing. When stars spill, a short descending twinkle of a few soft notes SHALL play. Neither SHALL sound like a warning or a punishment, and both SHALL respect the mute control.

#### Scenario: Spill
- **WHEN** the squid touches the jelly with stars in the meter
- **THEN** a soft low boing and a short descending twinkle play

#### Scenario: Muted
- **WHEN** the game is muted and the squid touches the jelly
- **THEN** no sound plays
