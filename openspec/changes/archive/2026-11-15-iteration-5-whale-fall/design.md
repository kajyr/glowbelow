## Context

- The zone graph, follower behaviour and saving already exist from iterations 2 and 3
- Home and Twilight are generated from a recipe and a seed, checked by the layout rules (`src/levels/generate.ts`, `rules.ts`, `generateValid.ts`)
- Nothing can be ridden yet: the nautilus is a friend to meet, not a ride
- The giant squid model is built in profile, facing right, at its own size; the game scales it up
- The album's speaker icon is hidden until `voice-recordings`

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Whale fall generated by the same system as the other zones
- A ride behaviour other friends can reuse (the nautilus later, maybe)
- Keep a model several times the squid's size cheap to animate

**Non-Goals:**
- No new mechanics beyond the ribs, waking and riding
- No game ending

## Decisions

### Whale fall is a generated zone
- A recipe like Twilight's: about 4 × 3 screens, seabed, rocks, sparkle trails, small fish schools, five starfish, one bubble-ring chain
- The skeleton and the giant squid are fixed anchors: placed first, along the seabed, then turned into keep-out areas for the rest
- Rules on top of the usual ones: the passage from home is clear, the gaps between ribs are at least two squid widths, and the space above the skull is free so the giant squid can rise
- Joined to home's left edge near the seabed; home's generator keeps that passage clear
- Alternative: hand-built `whaleFall.ts` (rejected: every other zone is generated)

### Ride as a shared "carrier" behaviour
- New in `src/game/carrier.ts`: a path, a seat point, hop-off on input
- The squid sits on the seat and follows the path; any steering input hops off
- The giant squid's path goes to "top of the world", resolved from the zone graph, so it reaches the reef in iteration 6 without changes here
- After setting the squid down, the giant squid sinks back to its spot in Whale fall; the squid swims back down by itself

### The reef gate
- The only place where a friend gates progress (agreed with the user)
- In iteration 5 there is no gate yet: the ride ends at the top of Twilight, which the squid can also reach by swimming
- Iteration 6 makes Twilight's top passage one-way (down only), so the ride becomes the only way up
- Soft by design: a full meter can't be failed, the whale song fills it, and dumbo points at the sleeping giant squid

### Giant squid animation by parts
- Body sways slowly; arms are separate nodes with a gentle wave; the offering arm is one tentacle node rotated toward the squid
- Eye open/closed by scaling the eye node

### Skeleton and fish are `.glb` models
- `tools/make_whale_skeleton.py` builds skull, spine and ribs in one model, each rib a named part; the game finds rib parts by name to light them and use them as note targets, with rib colliders in `src/levels/pieces.ts`
- `tools/make_small_fish.py` builds one tiny fish, instanced in small schools here and reused in later zones

### Rib notes on a fixed scale
- Ribs map to a pentatonic scale rising toward the head, reusing the sparkle note synth
- A pulse that reaches several ribs plays them nearest first, in a quick run

### Whale song
- Fires once, the first time every rib is lit, in any order (forgiving for a 6-year-old)
- After that, swimming along the lit ribs from tail to head plays it again
- Each time: a soft whale song, the whole skeleton glows brighter for a few seconds, a sparkle burst, and the glow meter fills
- Pure logic in `src/game/` (rib order, run detection) with Vitest tests

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The giant squid fills the screen] → keep it partly behind the skull and camera-aware scale; tune in playtest
- [A full meter is needed to wake it and the child doesn't know] → the giant squid stirs on small pulses, the whale song fills the meter, and dumbo points at it until it is awake
- [The tail-to-head replay triggers by accident] → it only needs to be nice when it happens; no cooldown needed beyond the song's length

## Migration Plan

No save format bump turned out to be needed (decided while building):
- Lit ribs go in the existing `lit` list (`rib-0` … `rib-6`), Whale fall starfish in `starfish`
- The giant squid is awake exactly when its stamp is found
- The first whale song has played exactly when every rib is lit (like the zone celebrations)
- An older save just has none of these ids: ribs dark, giant squid asleep
- Home and Twilight lay out as before (Whale fall has its own seed), so the layout version stays too
