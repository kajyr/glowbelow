## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Passage to Whale fall
Midnight home SHALL have a wide passage on its left side, near the seabed, leading into Whale fall, replacing the soft wall at that edge. The generator SHALL keep the passage free of rocks.

#### Scenario: Swimming left
- **WHEN** the squid swims through the left edge of Midnight home near the seabed
- **THEN** it continues into Whale fall

#### Scenario: Clear passage
- **WHEN** any generated home is checked
- **THEN** nothing blocks the passage to Whale fall
