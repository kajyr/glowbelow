## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Reef layout
The Sunlit reef SHALL be about 4 screens wide and 2 screens tall, directly above Twilight, generated from the world's seed, with a coral garden on the bottom and a shimmering water surface at the top that the squid cannot leave through. The squid SHALL be able to arrive by swimming up through Twilight's top passage or on the giant squid's arm, and SHALL be able to leave by swimming down into Twilight.

#### Scenario: Arriving by swimming
- **WHEN** the squid swims up through the top of Twilight
- **THEN** it comes up through the hole in the reef's seabed into the Sunlit reef

#### Scenario: Leaving
- **WHEN** the squid swims down through the bottom passage of the reef
- **THEN** it continues into the top of Twilight

#### Scenario: Surface
- **WHEN** the squid swims up to the surface
- **THEN** it is gently eased back down with no penalty (inside the sunbeam, the ending plays first: see "Ending in the sunbeam")
