### Full description (4000 max)

Glow Below is a peaceful little game about a tiny vampire squid in the deep sea, where it is very dark and very quiet. Until you glow.

Swim, catch drifting sparkles and give a pulse of light: sea plants bloom, jellyfish lanterns wake up, and the places you light stay lit. Dart through bubble rings for a burst of speed, and look for the shy starfish that wave back when you glow near them.

You are not alone down here. A dumbo octopus flaps along beside you, a phantom jelly drifts through the dark, a swarm of firefly squid follows you and makes shapes, and a very old nautilus cruises the Twilight waters. Glow at the ribs of an old whale skeleton and they play notes like a xylophone. Wake the giant squid with a big glow and ride its arm all the way up to the sunlit reef, where a shy cuttlefish hides among the corals.

Every friend you find goes into a sticker album, and your progress is saved on the device.

Made for young children (around 6):
• No dying, no timers, nothing lost
• No reading needed: icons, sound and animation only
• Big touch controls, made for tablets and phones
• Plays fully offline
• A grown-ups' corner (hold the button for a moment) for volume and starting over

One price, everything included: no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no links out, no data collected.
